🐉 Boss level · The traps

Five traps. Dodge them.

🪤The cheap trap

$146 looks nice. One bad year and it lapses. Fund it strong.

🌀Too many solves

Error? Solve one thing, type it in, then solve the next.

🎭The fake twin

Living benefits on term are not long term care. Small chunks, you don't pick the amount.

📈Promising the past

You can't illustrate past returns. Show 6.5 to 7%. Never promise more.

🚪Leaving early

Protector has a 15 year surrender charge. Gains above what they put in get taxed.

Beat the boss: fund it strong, solve one at a time, say it straight.
